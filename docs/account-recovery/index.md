# Account recovery guide

If Mammouth asks you to subscribe even though you have already paid, follow these two steps:

1. **Check that your payment was successful.** You received an invoice by email.
2. **Find the account linked to your subscription.** You are probably signed in with the wrong email address.

## 1. Check that your payment was successful

**An invoice is always sent by email when a payment succeeds**, whether it is for a new subscription or a renewal.

Look for the invoice in your inbox and spam folder. It is sent to:

- the email address of your subscribed account; or
- your billing email address, if you specifically provided a different one.

### If you found your invoice

Your payment was successful. Go to step 2.

### If you can't find your invoice

There are two possible explanations:

- **The payment did not go through.**
- **The invoice was sent to another email address.** Check your other inboxes, including their spam folders.

If you cannot find an invoice after checking all your inboxes, assume that the payment failed.

**To subscribe again:** sign in to the account you want to keep, then subscribe again from that same account.

### Special cases

::: tip If your payment was less than an hour ago and you received the invoice
Activating an account after a successful payment can take **up to 1 hour**. This delay is rare, but it can happen.

- Wait one hour before contacting us.
- After that, go to step 2.
:::

::: warning If your bank or banking app shows the payment, but you haven't found an invoice
Some banks and mobile banking apps may temporarily show a payment in your balance even though it failed.

- Wait **at least one hour** after the payment attempt.
- Make sure you have downloadable proof of payment. A screenshot of the transaction line in your mobile app is not proof of payment.
:::

::: tip If you paid with PayPal
The email address associated with your PayPal account may be different from:

- the email address of your subscribed account;
- the address where you received the invoice.

So don't rely only on your PayPal email address to find your invoice or account.
:::

## 2. Find the account linked to your subscription

Once you have found your invoice, your account email is usually the one shown on the invoice. However, your billing email address may not be the same as your account email address.

1. Sign out.
2. Sign in with one of your email addresses, starting with the one shown on your invoice.
3. Check whether you can access your subscription and conversations.
4. If not, repeat with each of your other email addresses until you find the right account.

### Special cases

::: warning If you use a Gmail address
On Mammouth, `john.campbell@gmail.com` and `johncampbell@gmail.com` are **two different accounts**.

- Try both versions of your address, with and without dots.
:::

::: tip If you received your invoice at an address that you can't use to sign in
Your billing email address may differ from your account email address.

- Don't stop at the email address on your invoice.
- Try all your other email addresses.
:::

::: tip Usually, a duplicate account has never had a subscription, and you are taken directly to the payment page without access to a conversation history. In some cases, you may see your old conversations while the platform asks you to subscribe again. This means you subscribed to two subscriptions on two separate accounts and cancelled only one of them: the one you are currently signed into.

- Sign out.
- Sign in with your other email addresses until you find the subscribed account.
:::

## If you still can't access your subscription

Contact **support@mammouth.ai** only if:

- you have found an invoice;
- more than one hour has passed since the payment;
- you have tried all your email addresses.

Include the following in your message:

- your invoice (*);
- the email address of the account you are currently signed into;
- the list of other email addresses you tried.

(*) Can't find your invoice? Send downloadable proof of payment instead (a screenshot of the transaction line in your mobile app is not proof of payment). Our team will try to find your email address, but we cannot guarantee that we will succeed.

Mention that you followed this guide.

As a last resort, you can revoke the direct debit authorization through your bank, or block your payment method if necessary. This will effectively stop the subscription due to non-payment.

## If you have lost access to your Google account

We cannot restore access to your Google account.

- Contact Google Support and follow its recovery process, which may take a few days.
- If you cannot recover your account, cancel your subscription through your bank to prevent renewal.

**If you cannot cancel through your bank**, contact **support@mammouth.ai** and provide:

- your first and last name;
- your latest invoice, or the amount and date of your last payment;
- the last 4 digits of your bank card;
- the email address of the account you can no longer access.

We will cancel the renewal for you.
